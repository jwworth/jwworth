Hi, I'm Jake. 👋

I'm a senior software engineer with over a decade of experience building and
shipping production systems with React, Ruby, Elixir, Python, and PostgreSQL in
small, high-trust teams.

Outside of software, I make music, meditate, and go on long walks with a heavy
backpack.

## What I do

### Engineering leadership

I work full time as a senior software engineer, helping teams ship cool things
while growing their skills, judgment, and confidence.

I'm available [for consulting work](https://www.jakeworth.com/consulting) and
publicly release software
[projects and talks](https://www.jakeworth.com/projects-and-talks) as often as I
can.

I share what I'm learning in two places:

- [Posts](https://www.jakeworth.com/posts) — Longer essays
- [TIL](https://www.jakeworth.com/tils) — Short, technical daily learnings

### Mentoring & teaching

Formally and informally, I jump at opportunities to help people grow. I teach
bootcamps, mentor 1:1, and work with dozens of engineers at different stages of
their careers.

### Community

engaged, highly technical group helping strengthen the Maine tech scene.
